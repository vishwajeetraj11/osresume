## Design Context

### Users
Job seekers who want a professional resume fast — ideally in under 15 minutes. They arrive with urgency and a specific outcome in mind (getting interviews), not to explore or be entertained. They are on desktop; the editor intentionally does not support mobile. They need to trust the output looks polished enough to send to employers.

### Brand Personality
**Sharp. Bold. Minimal.**

OS Resume is a free, open-source tool that earns trust through restraint — not decoration. The voice is direct and confident. The product positions itself against complexity, not against professionalism. Copy like "Oversimplifying Resume Building Process" signals competence without being corporate or cold.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone**: Purposefully minimal. Every element earns its place. No gradients for decoration, no shadow theatrics, no illustration-heavy sections.
- **Color**: Teal-green primary — target `#0d9e84` (deeper than the current `#1abc9c`) for more weight and confidence while keeping brand recognition. Teal was chosen deliberately: blue (LinkedIn/Indeed) is too generic, teal signals growth and new beginnings which maps to a job search. Used decisively — CTAs, sidebar, scrollbar — not scattered. Background stays near-white (`#f9fafb`). Typography in grays (`#808080`, `#73808D`, `#101214`). When updating the primary, update `tailwind.config.js` (`primary`) and the scrollbar thumb in `styles/global.css`.
- **Typography**: Space Grotesk for all project UI (not resume templates). Body at weight 300–400; 500–600 for labels and emphasis; 700 for headings. Space Grotesk was chosen for its distinctive geometric character that reads "crafted by someone who cares" — more personality than Inter, less corporate than Poppins. Hierarchy through weight and size, not color noise. Resume templates retain their own font system (`customStyles.font`) and are unaffected.
- **Anti-reference: NOT like Canva.** Avoid: decorative template carousels, playful color palettes, graphic-heavy layouts, drag-anywhere freedom, icon overload, or anything that feels like a design playground. OS Resume is a tool, not a creative suite.
- **Theme**: Light mode only. No dark mode in scope.

### Design Principles
1. **Restraint over decoration** — if an element doesn't communicate something useful, remove it. Whitespace is structure, not emptiness.
2. **Confidence through consistency** — the teal accent appears in predictable, high-signal places (primary action, active state, brand mark). It should never feel arbitrary.
3. **Speed as UX** — interactions should feel immediate. Avoid loading states where possible; when unavoidable, use skeleton loaders not spinners.
4. **The resume is the hero** — the editor UI recedes so the live resume preview commands attention. Sidebars, drawers, and controls should feel like supporting infrastructure, not competing surfaces.
5. **Professional, not corporate** — sharp and clean, but approachable. The open-source roots matter: this feels built by someone who cares, not a SaaS product trying to upsell you.
